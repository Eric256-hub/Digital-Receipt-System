What the system offers

Everything you need to manage your receipts.

A focused set of tools designed to make transaction recording and receipt management easier for small businesses.

Digital Receipt Generation

Create organised receipts with unique reference numbers for completed transactions.

Transaction Recording

Record customer details, multiple items, quantities and prices in one transaction.

Automatic Calculations

Calculate individual item totals and the overall transaction amount.

Receipt Printing

Open a receipt preview and print it or save it as a PDF using your browser.

Transaction History

Search and retrieve earlier transaction records using receipt details.

Business Management

Set up your business profile and manage authorised access to its records.
